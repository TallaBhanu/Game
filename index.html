Hyderabad · Deccan · Anno 1518

Golconda

Sultan Quli Qutb-ul-Mulk has declared his independence, and the old Kakatiya mud fort on the shepherd's hill is being reborn in granite. Masons haul stone up earthen ramps, merchants weigh pearls and diamonds in the bazaar, and every day the Sultan climbs the hill to see his walls rise.

You arrive at Fateh Darwaza with sixty gold huns and a curious eye.
